Will they pay MEQAT?

This is the demand-validation plan in ordinary language. Meqat sells complete Umrah Packages. A Pilgrim pays Meqat, not a travel desk. We do not yet know if anyone in Toronto will do that. This is how we find out, in eight weeks, with no ad spend. It is a plan to run. It is not the running.

Pass

What "pass" means

Interac received · Meqat

5 Groups

Distinct households. Money in the account. That is the only pass.

How we count

  • Households, not seats. Two Bookings from the same family count once. Report seats on the side.
  • A refund later still counts, including the one-day free cancel.
  • A Booking with no phone call still counts. The cleaner read that they used the site themselves.
  • Ten Groups is more confident, not a second gate. If we hit five before Week 8 ends, we can stop.

If Week 8 ends short of five, sort what happened

  1. People visited, almost no calls, no payments The post or the group was wrong. Write another experiment.
  2. They started Interac and the money never arrived Checkout is the leak. Write another experiment.
  3. They got on the call, then paid a desk Pause "Pilgrim pays Meqat." Do not conclude that GTA does not buy Umrah.
  4. They got on the call and nobody paid anyone We do not have enough calls to read a rate. About 70 completed calls would be the first honest look. This window will not get there.

Not a pass

  • × A row in the database, "payment started," a Package page view, a Consultation, a nice interview.
  • × A cousin doing you a favour.
  • × A Service Provider pushing someone who was already buying from that desk.
  • × They take a Consultation on Meqat and then pay the Service Provider off the site. Assurance only attaches to an on-platform Booking.

Bets

Offer

What we offer

  1. Look

    Complete Umrah Packages on meqat.ca. Flight, hotel, what's included, who the Imam is.

  2. Talk if you want

    Book a Consultation on Cal.com meqat/30min.

  3. Pay if you want the trip

    Book and pay Meqat.

That is what the site can actually do. There is no compare widget. "Look at the Packages in one place" is honest. "Side-by-side compare tool" is not.

Do not promise cheapest, Hajj, we will shop for you, book it yourself with no Package, a deposit match, or a Commission rate. The Commission rate is still an open product question.

Send people to meqat.ca/packages, not the homepage. The homepage still says "Find Your Clarity" and still shows expired Packages. Do not change the app this window.

Before Week 1, /packages needs at least one Toronto Package with seats left whose departure is far enough after Week 1 for visa and flights. Recheck the week you start. A conversation with a Service Provider about a later departure is setup. An empty shelf is not a demand miss.

Score

How we keep score

The site can see page views. It cannot see "they compared," "they booked a Consultation," or "they paid." We will not build those events this window. We write paid by hand when Interac lands.

What happened How we know Does it pass?
They visited /packages PostHog page view, with UTM No
They opened a Package Page view on /packages/[slug] No
They looked at more than one Two different Package pages the same day from the same UTM, or they said so No
They booked a Consultation Hand log from Cal.com No. Strong lead.
They opened the Booking wizard Page view on /bookings No
A Booking row exists Database No
They clicked pay paymentInitiationEmailSent No. Many of these and no money is leak 2.
Money arrived Interac landed, row in the notebook Yes, toward 5 Groups

Rates are a soft guide, not the gate. Visit to Booking 0.2% to 1.0%. Visit to Consultation 0.5% to 2%. Consultation to Booking 15% to 40%. Do not A/B those rates. We will not have 70 Consultations or 1,500 visits.

Notebook row: date paid, Group (one household), Package, seats (report, do not count), CAD received, Consultation yes or no and date, named channel, stuffing check, looked at more than one Package, refund later (still counts).

At two Consultations a week the ceiling is 16 calls. At 15% to 40% that is roughly 2 to 6 paid Bookings if every slot fills and converts. Five Groups needs those slots almost full and conversion at the high end, or some Bookings with no Consultation.

Week 1 · first name

Finish any open setup. Message 1 of the 5. Do not post in a Facebook group yet. If the live Package is too soon for visa and flights from this week, talk to a Service Provider about a later departure.

Week 2 · remaining named people

Finish the five names. Start listening in Pakistani in Toronto - GTA. Hold Consultations if anyone booked.

Week 3 · one reply

One reply to a live "which group?" thread, or one post if allowed. Hold Consultations. Log everything.

Week 4 · stay or move

If Channel 2 produced a conversation, stay. If it was silent, Channel 3 reply-only.

Weeks 5 and 6 · stay

Stay on the channel that produced conversations. Add Channel 4 only if both 2 and 3 were silent. Do not add a fifth.

Week 6 is the same stretch, not a new channel week.

Week 7 · follow up

No new channels. Follow up people who looked or consulted and did not pay. If they started Interac, follow up.

Week 8 · stop and score

Stop looking for new people. Score the notebook. Apply the miss list under Pass.

Weekly session: score last week, do or prepare the next conversation in the room, write next week's one named action. A person or a group, with a real name.
